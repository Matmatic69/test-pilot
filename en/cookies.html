Cookies Policy

This policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies on our website.

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device when you visit a website, allowing information to be remembered (e.g., preferences, session).

2. Types of cookies used

  • Essential (strictly necessary): required for site operation (security, navigation, session...).
  • Analytics (optional): only activated with your consent.
  • Functional (optional): improve experience (e.g., remember some preferences) – consent-based.

3. Consent management

On your first visit, a banner lets you accept or refuse optional cookies. You can change your choice at any time:

Manage my cookie preferences

4. Retention

Durations vary by cookie. Consent choices are kept up to 12 months, then renewed.
